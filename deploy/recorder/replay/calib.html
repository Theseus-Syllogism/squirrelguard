Camera map calibration

Enter the camera's position (in Google Maps: right-click the spot where the camera is mounted, click the coordinates to copy them) and load the aerial photo. Then click a point in the camera frame and the same point on the aerial photo; repeat for 6–10 points spread across the view that sit on the ground (path corners, driveway edges, post bases). The aerial photo and heights come straight from USGS to this browser.
